💖 About Cuddlecot

Cuddlecot is a cozy and modern online shopping platform designed to provide a smooth and delightful shopping experience. Our goal is to bring comfort, quality, and affordability together in one place.

Our Collections Include:

  • 👗 Trendy Fashion Wear
  • 🧸 Soft Lifestyle Products
  • 🛏️ Cozy Home Essentials
  • 🎁 Cute Gift Items

At Cuddlecot, we focus on high-quality materials, especially soft and breathable cotton fabrics. We ensure every product is carefully selected to provide maximum comfort and durability.

This application is built using Angular for frontend and connected to a database using DBMS concepts, ensuring proper data storage, user management, and order tracking.
